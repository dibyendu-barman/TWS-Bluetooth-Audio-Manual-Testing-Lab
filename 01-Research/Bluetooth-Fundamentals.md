# Bluetooth Fundamentals

## 1. Bluetooth Overview

Bluetooth is a short-range wireless communication technology used for communication between compatible devices.

For this project, Bluetooth is the communication technology used between the host device and the TWS audio device.

## 2. Bluetooth Profiles

### A2DP

Advanced Audio Distribution Profile.

Primary area:

- Audio streaming
- Music playback
- Stereo audio

### AVRCP

Audio/Video Remote Control Profile.

Primary area:

- Media control
- Play
- Pause
- Next
- Previous

### HFP

Hands-Free Profile.

Primary area:

- Voice calls
- Hands-free communication
- Microphone
- Speaker

## 3. Bluetooth Low Energy

Bluetooth LE is a Bluetooth technology designed for low-power communication.

Important concepts:

- GAP
- GATT
- ATT
- Services
- Characteristics
- Descriptors

## 4. GAP

Generic Access Profile.

Important concepts:

- Device discovery
- Advertising
- Connection procedures
- Security

## 5. GATT

Generic Attribute Profile.

Important concepts:

- Services
- Characteristics
- Descriptors
- Client
- Server

## 6. Pairing

Pairing establishes the relationship/security information required for Bluetooth communication.

## 7. Connection

Connection establishes an active communication link between Bluetooth devices.

## 8. QA Perspective

Important testing areas include:

- Discovery

- Reconnection
- Audio streaming
